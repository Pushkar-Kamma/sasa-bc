About SASA

Celebrating South Asian culture, building friendships, and creating a welcoming community at Bellevue College.

Our Mission

SASA, the South Asian Student Association at Bellevue College, brings students together to celebrate South Asian cultures and build a stronger campus community.

We create a welcoming space to meet people, share traditions, and enjoy campus life together. Whether you grew up with these traditions or are curious to learn something new, you are welcome here.

Through cultural celebrations, social gatherings, and shared food, we make it easier for students to find community, make friends, and feel at home at Bellevue College.

Make memories

Come as you are

You do not need to be South Asian or know anyone in the club to join. Bring your curiosity, meet the community, and take part in whatever feels right for you.

Our events are a chance to connect across backgrounds and celebrate together, with good company, fun activities, and free food at many gatherings.
